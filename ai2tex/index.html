FAQ

Questions? We've got answers.

Which 3ds Max versions are supported?

ai2tex is tested and supported on 3ds Max 2025 or newer.

Is ai2tex really free?

Yes. The tool is free to download and use. An email address is not required.

Do I need to provide an email to download it?

No. Email signup is completely optional.

What does it transfer?

Currently, ai2tex bakes Diffuse / Base Color. Normal, Roughness, Metallic, Opacity, Emission and AO are not currently transferred.

Does it work with Corona?

The script can be used in workflows with Corona and other renderers, but it temporarily switches to the Default Scanline Renderer during baking and restores the previous renderer afterward.

Can I use it with non-AI models?

Yes. The workflow is not limited to AI-generated assets. It can transfer texture information between compatible source and target meshes.

Can I bake multiple models at once?

Not currently. One Source and one Target are processed at a time.
